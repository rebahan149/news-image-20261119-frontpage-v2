Shenzhen · Summit Reported

Trump, Xi and Putin take one table for the first time

The three leaders met at Shenzhen’s Kylin Villa beside the APEC summit, after a Trump–Xi bilateral. Washington put nuclear arms control forward; the Kremlin said Ukraine topped its list. Kyiv was not in the room, and no joint statement followed.

2
Worldwide · Games Reported

GTA VI is out at last, 13 years after the one before

The game unlocked at midnight in New Zealand and rolled west all day. Reviews average 99 from more than 140 critics, and 2.3 million people watched streams within hours. It is on PS5 and Xbox only — no PC version — at $79.99.

3
New York · Markets Forecast

Anthropic steps onto the Nasdaq with the biggest float on record

The maker of the Claude models sold 980m shares at $83 — priced on Wednesday, above its range — raising $81.3bn and passing SpaceX’s old mark. The shares, ticker ANT, were due to open with the New York bell.

4
The Hague · Law Reported

World Court: Myanmar did commit genocide

Judges ruled 9 to 6 that the 2017 campaign meant to destroy the Rohingya, counting some 10,000 dead. A first for the court. No appeal lies.

5
Antalya · Climate Forecast

COP31 runs into its last 48 hours

Ministers inherit 21 unsettled issues. Fresh draft texts on finance, adaptation and a just transition landed at dawn; the money chapter is still in brackets.

6
Odisha · Storm Forecast

Akvan nears; 204,070 listed to evacuate

Odisha has listed 204,070 people for possible evacuation and already moved about 50,000. Schools in six districts are shut for three days. Landfall: Friday noon.

7
Crimea · War Reported

Kerch Bridge hit a third time in four days

Neptune missiles struck the rail span overnight and halted the trains, Kyiv said. Tuesday’s strike had already dropped 227 metres of road deck into the strait.

8
Gaza · Ceasefire Reported

Israel calls its Gaza tank raid finished

The army said Thursday its push past the Yellow Line, begun Wednesday near Bureij and Nuseirat, is complete. The Salah al-Din road has reopened.

9
Riyadh · Gulf Claim

Coalition: Houthi missile stopped over Riyadh

Sirens sounded before dawn and falling debris caused damage, the Saudi-led coalition said. A Houthi claim to have hit the airport is unverified.

10
Seoul · Education Reported

551,864 sit the exam that stops a nation

Offices opened at ten and jets were hushed for the listening test. A nationwide phone alert exposed 22 candidates who had kept their phones.

11
Jakarta · Law Reported

Indonesia passes its seize-first asset law

After an 18-year wait, parliament voted at 09:30 to let the state seize suspect assets of Rp1bn or more before any conviction, by court order.

12
Paris · Politics Reported

Greens swing to Mélenchon, and split the left

Marine Tondelier’s move toward the Mélenchon camp drew a Thursday protest from Raphaël Glucksmann. Socialist chief Olivier Faure concedes the momentum.

13
Bogotá · Labour Reported

Unions hold a national strike in Colombia

Teachers and workers marched in Bogotá, Medellín and Cali against the ‘Economic Rescue’ tax bill — even after food VAT was dropped.

14
Canberra · Economy Reported

Australia’s jobless rate climbs to 4.9%

A five-year high. The economy shed 43,500 jobs in October against forecasts of a 25,000 gain; 753,700 people are now out of work.

15
Florida · Weather Forecast

Floods close South Florida schools again

Up to 15 inches of rain fell on Hollywood in a day. Four counties closed on Thursday; Volusia stays shut on Friday too, as the water lingers.
